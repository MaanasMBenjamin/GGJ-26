# 🎭 Reclamation: 1947 – GGJ 2026 Prototype

## 📌 Overview

**Reclamation: 1947** is a stealth-based 2D prototype game developed during Global Game Jam 2026. The project was created within a limited time frame by a newly formed group of developers, artists, and designers.

The game was built around the jam theme **“Mask”** and integrates symbolism inspired by Indian cultural elements, particularly the meanings behind the colors of the Indian flag.

---

## 🧭 Story & Concept

The game follows a character attempting to reclaim cultural artifacts that were taken from India during the British colonial period. In the game’s fictional setting, these artifacts are being stored inside a heavily secured laboratory operated by colonial-era scientists.

Players must infiltrate the laboratory, bypass advanced security systems, and strategically use special masks that grant unique abilities. Each mask reflects symbolic meanings derived from the colors of the Indian flag, connecting gameplay mechanics with cultural themes.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

Players explore a guarded laboratory environment while avoiding enemies, security systems, and detection zones. Progression depends on using masks effectively and switching abilities based on the situation.

Each mask provides a unique gameplay advantage and introduces strategic decision-making through cooldowns and risk mechanics.

---

## 🎭 Mask Ability System

### 🟢 Green Mask – Growth & Faith
- Provides a **Speed Boost Ability**
- Allows faster movement and quick navigation through danger zones

### ⚪ White Mask – Peace & Truth
- Provides an **Invisibility Ability**
- Allows players to temporarily avoid enemies and security detection

### 🟠 Saffron Mask – Sacrifice
- Introduces a **High Risk – High Reward Mechanic**
- Reveals the true colors and abilities of all masks during the next blink cycle
- If the player fails to switch to another mask before cooldown ends, the player dies

---

## ✨ Unique Gameplay Mechanic – Mask Blink System

Masks appear inside rooms using a blinking visibility system.

- All masks appear **pink by default**
- The true color and ability of a mask is revealed only after equipping it
- Prevents players from always selecting safe or preferred abilities
- Encourages exploration, experimentation, and risk management

Each mask includes a **6-second cooldown** once equipped.
